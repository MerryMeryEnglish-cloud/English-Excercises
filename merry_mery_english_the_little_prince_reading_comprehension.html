Adapted Passage (160 Words)

Once, the Little Prince met a wild fox in the desert. "Come and play with me," said the prince. "I am very sad."

"I cannot play with you," said the fox. "I am not tamed."

"What does 'tamed' mean?" asked the prince.

"It means to create bonds," explained the fox. "To me, you are just a little boy like a hundred thousand other little boys. And I do not need you. But if you tame me, we will need each other. You will be unique in the world to me."

The prince thought about his rose on his small planet. "I think a flower tamed me," he said softly.

The fox smiled and said: "Here is my secret. It is very simple: You can only see well with the heart. What is essential is invisible to the eyes. It is the time you spent with your rose that makes her so important."
